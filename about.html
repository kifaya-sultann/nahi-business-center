About Nahi Business Center

Nahi Business Center was established with a clear vision — to provide businesses of all sizes with a professional, secure, and fully equipped environment to operate and grow.

Located in the heart of Adama, Ethiopia, we offer a range of flexible office and commercial spaces designed to meet the needs of startups, growing businesses, and established enterprises alike.

Our commitment is simple: give every business the foundation it needs to succeed.
